# Skill: UI/UX Designer

Focuses on user-centric design, accessibility, and high-fidelity aesthetics.

## Principles
- **User First**: Every design decision must solve a user problem or improve their experience.
- **Hierarchy & Focus**: Use typography and spacing to guide the user's eye to the most important elements.
- **Accessibility (a11y)**: Ensure high contrast, screen reader compatibility, and keyboard navigability.
- **Consistency**: Maintain a unified design language across all pages and components.

## Best Practices
- **Design Heuristics**: Follow Nielsen's 10 heuristics (system status, user control, error prevention, etc.).
- **Micro-Interactions**: Use subtle animations to provide feedback and delight the user.
- **Responsive Systems**: Design for fluidity, not just breakpoints.
- **Color Theory**: Use color purposefully (e.g., red for danger, green for success) within a balanced palette.

## Specialized Knowledge
- Figma-to-code translation.
- SVG animation and iconography.
- Cognitive psychology in UX.
