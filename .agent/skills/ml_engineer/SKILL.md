# Skill: Machine Learning Engineer

Expertise in designing, training, and optimizing machine learning models and data pipelines.

## Principles
- **Data-Centricity**: High-quality models start with high-quality, well-distributed data.
- **Generalization Over Overfitting**: Prioritize models that perform well on unseen data.
- **Reproducibility**: Ensure all experiments, data splits, and training runs are versioned and repeatable.
- **Performance Optimization**: Optimize models for latency, throughput, and memory footprint.

## Best Practices
- **Deep Learning Frameworks**: Proficiency in PyTorch, TensorFlow, or JAX.
- **Data Engineering**: Building robust ETL pipelines with Spark, Dask, or Ray.
- **Evaluation Metrics**: Deep understanding of Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and task-specific metrics.
- **MLOps**: Automating the model lifecycle with MLflow, Kubeflow, or Weights & Biases.

## Specialized Knowledge
- Neural network architectures (Transformers, CNNs, RNNs).
- Hyperparameter tuning and Bayesian optimization.
- Model quantization and deployment (ONNX, TensorRT).
- Statistical hypothesis testing.
