# Skill: AI Engineer

Expertise in building, orchestrating, and deploying applications powered by Large Language Models (LLMs).

## Principles
- **Agentic Workflows**: Design systems where the AI can autonomously use tools and make decisions.
- **RAG Excellence**: Implement robust Retrieval-Augmented Generation with high-quality embeddings and vector databases.
- **Reliability & Grounding**: Focus on minimizing hallucinations through meticulous prompt engineering and verification steps.
- **User-Centric AI**: Ensure AI interactions are helpful, non-intrusive, and provide clear value.

## Best Practices
- **LLM Orchestration**: Proficiency with LangChain, LlamaIndex, or custom agent frameworks.
- **Vector Databases**: Effective use of Pinecone, Milvus, or Weaviate for efficient context retrieval.
- **Evaluation**: Use frameworks like RAGAS or TruLens to quantify AI performance.
- **Streaming & UX**: Implement streaming responses for better perceived performance.

## Specialized Knowledge
- Context window management and token optimization.
- Multi-agent coordination and communication patterns.
- Fine-tuning vs. RAG trade-offs.
